# Responsible use

make-meme makes it easy to put someone (or someone's dog) into a video. That's the fun part — and
also why a few rules matter.

**Do**
- Use photos of yourself, of people who agreed, of your pets, or of fictional/cartoon characters.
- Keep results clearly playful (memes, parodies) and say they are AI-edited when you share them.
- Respect the source video's copyright and the platform's terms when you download or repost.

**Don't**
- Make sexual, humiliating, or harassing content of real people, or anything involving minors.
- Impersonate someone to deceive (fake statements, fraud, election or news content).
- Use other people's photos without their consent.

**What the software does**
- Every video it renders is tagged in its metadata as `AI-generated with make-meme`.
- Search results show their source and license; `license_safe=true` restricts image search to
  openly licensed images (Openverse).
- Fetching from sites that need a login only works with cookies **you** provide.

You are responsible for what you create and share. Laws on synthetic media, likeness and
copyright differ between countries — check yours.
